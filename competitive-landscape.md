## Summary

This pull request adds a dedicated competitive landscape research page for the Air Traffic Management Market.

The new research file focuses on how competition differs across ATM technology layers rather than treating the market as a single-product industry.

## What This Adds

- Competitive positioning of major ATM vendors
- Surveillance and radar landscape
- Digital tower competition
- Communication and flight-data systems
- Cloud-native ATM development
- U-space and drone traffic management
- Cybersecurity as an emerging competitive factor
- Installed-base and certification advantages
- Competitive outlook through 2031

## Market Context

The Air Traffic Management Market is estimated at approximately USD 5.33 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach around USD 7.78 billion by 2031.

The research examines how market value may increasingly shift from traditional hardware toward software, digital towers, automation, satellite surveillance, cybersecurity and recurring services.
